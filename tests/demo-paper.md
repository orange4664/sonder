# A Graph-Based Method for Node Classification

## Abstract
Graph neural networks have emerged as a powerful tool for node classification.
We show that our method achieves significant improvements over existing
approaches in terms of accuracy.

## Introduction
It is important to note that most existing methods rely on a preconstructed
graph, which is often noisy and incomplete. To address this, we propose a
novel graph construction strategy based on semantic similarity. Our results
demonstrate that this strategy is better than the baseline.
